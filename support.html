Common Questions

Quick answers to common SocialWorkBD account and marketplace questions.

Why can my account be restricted?
Accounts may be restricted when activity violates marketplace rules, communication policies, payment rules, or other platform requirements.
Can I request a review after suspension?
Yes. If you believe a moderation decision was incorrect, submit a review request with a clear explanation of your situation.
Can I share external contact information in messages?
SocialWorkBD messaging is intended for marketplace communication. Keep project discussions and transactions on the platform and avoid moving business communication to external platforms.
Will my Account ID change after a restriction?
Your Account ID is intended to remain associated with your account. A moderation action does not automatically create a new identity.
How should I report another user?
Use the report option available in the relevant conversation or marketplace flow. Provide accurate information so the support team can review the situation.
