Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how SocialWorkBD may collect, use, store and protect information when you use our global freelance marketplace.

Last updated: September 2026
Your privacy matters. SocialWorkBD is designed to collect and use information reasonably necessary to operate accounts, profiles, jobs, proposals, marketplace communication, support and security features.

1. Introduction

SocialWorkBD is a global online marketplace that connects clients and independent freelancers.

This Privacy Policy describes the types of information that may be processed when you create an account, maintain a profile, post or apply for jobs, communicate with other users, request support or otherwise use SocialWorkBD.

By using SocialWorkBD, you acknowledge that your information may be processed as described in this policy, subject to applicable law.

2. Information We Collect

The information collected may depend on which SocialWorkBD features you use.

Account Information

  • Name or display name.
  • Email address.
  • Firebase authentication identifier.
  • Account role, such as freelancer or client.
  • Account creation and update information.

Profile Information

  • Professional title.
  • Skills and categories.
  • Biography or professional description.
  • Experience information.
  • Availability.
  • Location information that you choose to publish.
  • Hourly rate or other professional information.

Marketplace Information

  • Jobs you post.
  • Proposals you submit.
  • Project-related information.
  • Messages and marketplace communications.
  • Support requests and account review requests.

3. How We Use Information

SocialWorkBD may use information to operate and improve the marketplace.

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and maintain account security.
  • Display freelancer and client profiles.
  • Publish and manage jobs.
  • Process and display proposals.
  • Enable marketplace communication.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Investigate reports, abuse and policy violations.
  • Detect and help prevent fraud or unauthorized activity.
  • Maintain and improve platform functionality.
  • Meet applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

4. Public Profiles

SocialWorkBD may allow users to create public professional profiles so that other marketplace users can discover suitable freelancers or clients.

Information that you choose to place on a public profile, such as your professional title, skills, biography, experience, availability, location or hourly rate, may be visible to other users.

Do not publish sensitive personal information in fields intended for public display.

5. Marketplace Data

When you use SocialWorkBD marketplace features, information related to your activity may be stored so that the platform can provide those features.

Jobs

Job titles, descriptions, budgets, skills, delivery requirements, status and related client information may be stored and displayed according to the marketplace flow.

Proposals

Proposal information submitted by freelancers may be stored and made available to the relevant client and marketplace systems.

Messages

Messages and other communications may be stored to provide messaging functionality, maintain conversation history, investigate reports and protect the marketplace.

6. Firebase & Service Providers

SocialWorkBD currently uses Google Firebase services for important application functionality, including authentication and cloud database features.

Depending on the services enabled in the project, Firebase may process information necessary to provide authentication, database, hosting, security or related infrastructure.

Firebase documentation explains that customers using Firebase are generally responsible for their obligations concerning the personal data they provide to Firebase, while Google may act as a processor or service provider depending on the applicable legal framework. 1

Third-party service providers may process information on behalf of SocialWorkBD where necessary to operate the platform or provide requested services.

7. Information Sharing

SocialWorkBD does not intend to sell personal information simply because you use the marketplace.

Information may be shared or made available in limited circumstances, including:

  • With other users when you intentionally publish information through a public profile or marketplace feature.
  • With service providers that help operate the platform.
  • When necessary to investigate fraud, abuse, security incidents or policy violations.
  • When required by applicable law or valid legal process.
  • When necessary to protect users, SocialWorkBD, or the rights and safety of others.

8. Security

SocialWorkBD uses reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect account and marketplace data.

However, no internet service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should use strong passwords, avoid sharing login credentials and notify SocialWorkBD if they suspect unauthorized account activity.

9. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain marketplace records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, enforce policies, maintain security or comply with legal obligations.

Some information may need to remain available after an account is closed when retention is reasonably necessary for legal, security, fraud-prevention or dispute-resolution purposes.

10. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information.

  • Request access to certain personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion where legally available.
  • Request information about how your data is processed.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing where applicable.

You can also update information in your SocialWorkBD profile where the relevant editing feature is available.

Privacy requests can be submitted through the Help & Support page.

11. Children

SocialWorkBD is intended for users who are legally permitted to use online freelance and marketplace services in their jurisdiction.

If applicable law requires a minimum age or parental authorization for a particular service, that requirement applies.

If we learn that personal information has been collected from a person who is not permitted to use the service, we may take appropriate steps to remove or restrict that information.

12. International Users

SocialWorkBD is intended for a global audience. Depending on the services used and infrastructure involved, information may be processed or stored in countries other than the country where you live.

International data processing may be subject to the requirements and safeguards applicable to the relevant service providers and jurisdictions.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

SocialWorkBD may update this Privacy Policy when the platform, data practices, services or applicable legal requirements change.

The updated version will be published on this page and the update date may be changed accordingly.

Continued use of SocialWorkBD after an update may indicate that you have been presented with the revised policy, subject to applicable law.

14. Privacy Contact

If you have a privacy question, want to request correction or deletion, or have a concern about how your information is handled, please contact SocialWorkBD through the Help & Support page.

Go to Help & Support

Please avoid sending passwords, authentication codes, payment-card numbers or other unnecessary sensitive information through a support request.
