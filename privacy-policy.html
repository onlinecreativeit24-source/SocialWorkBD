Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 5, 2026

1. Introduction

SocialWorkBD ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform, including our website and mobile application (collectively, the "Service").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Registration: Email, password, name, phone number, profile photo, location
  • Profile Information: Skills, bio, work experience, portfolio links, education
  • Communication: Messages between freelancers and clients, project details, proposals
  • Payment Information: PayPal transaction references (we do NOT store payment card data)
  • Support Requests: Any communications with our support team

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, browser type
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, clicks, search queries
  • Location Data: IP address (general location), country
  • Analytics: Google Analytics for site performance monitoring

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for:

  • Providing and maintaining the Service
  • Processing payments and verifying transactions
  • Sending transactional emails (account updates, payment confirmations)
  • Detecting and preventing fraud and security violations
  • Improving and personalizing the Service
  • Complying with legal obligations
  • Responding to user inquiries and support requests
  • Marketing and promotional communications (with your consent)

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information:

  • Encryption: All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Firebase Security: Data at rest is protected by Google Firebase security infrastructure
  • Access Control: Only authorized employees have access to sensitive data
  • Payment Security: We do NOT store payment card information. PayPal handles all payment processing
  • Regular Security Audits: We conduct periodic security reviews

Note: While we implement security measures, no system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

5. Data Retention

  • Account Data: Retained while your account is active
  • Transaction Records: Retained for 7 years (legal/tax requirements)
  • Communications: Retained for dispute resolution and service improvement
  • Deleted Accounts: You can request permanent data deletion (except legally required records)

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase: Cloud hosting, authentication, database - Privacy Policy
  • PayPal: Payment processing - Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics: Usage analytics - Privacy Policy

These third parties may collect information according to their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations)
  • Right to Data Portability: Request your data in a portable format
  • Right to Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from promotional communications

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@socialworkbd.com

8. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

For EU Users (GDPR): We comply with the General Data Protection Regulation. Your personal data is processed based on your consent or contractual necessity. You have the right to withdraw consent at any time.

For California Users (CCPA): You have specific rights regarding your personal information. You can request disclosure, deletion, and opt-out of data sales. We do not sell personal information.

9. Children's Privacy

SocialWorkBD is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child without parental consent, we will delete such information immediately.
